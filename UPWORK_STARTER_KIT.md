## Short proposal template

Replace the bracketed sections with details from the actual job. Remove any statement that is not true for that engagement.

> You’re looking to [specific user outcome], and the main implementation challenge appears to be [constraint in the job post].
>
> I worked on [relevant project], where I implemented [specific related responsibility]. Here’s the closest example: [one relevant project link].
>
> I’d start by [first concrete step], then deliver [reviewable first milestone]. Before estimating, I’d like to clarify [one question that affects scope].
>
> Happy to discuss the requirements and next steps.

## Three focused starting engagements

Scope and price these only after requirements are understood. These are draft offers, not published listings or promises.

- **Next.js feature milestone:** one agreed user flow, connected to the necessary API and data model, with a reviewable demo and setup notes.
- **AI integration proof of concept:** one defined input/output workflow connected to an agreed AI provider, with agreed sample cases and notes on usage and limitations.
- **React Native sync review:** inspect the existing offline flow, reproduce an agreed failure, and provide prioritized findings; scope implementation separately.

## Stronger proof to collect

- A 60–90 second recording showing one actual product flow and your contribution.
- Permission-approved screenshots attached to the matching Upwork portfolio item.
- Genuine reviews linked to the relevant completed work.
- Measured before/after results where you have supporting records.

Keep employer work explicitly attributed to your role in that team. Avoid claiming that enterprise customers hired you directly.
